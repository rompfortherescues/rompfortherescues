Privacy Policy

Romp for the Rescues, LLC
Effective date: August 31, 2026
Last updated: August 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Romp for the Rescues, LLC handles information you provide when you register, volunteer, make a payment, or otherwise use rompfortherescues.org.

In short: We do not keep or share the information we collect with anyone outside our staff, except in the emails we use to communicate with the person who provided that information.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect your name, email address, telephone number, event or volunteer preferences, and any information you include in a form.

How We Use Information

We use the information you provide to communicate with you about your registration, payment, volunteering, or event participation. We do not keep that information after it has been used for that communication.

Sharing Information

We do not share your information with anyone outside our staff, except where it appears in emails used to communicate with you. Those emails may be received by the staff members responsible for responding to you or coordinating an event.

Payment Information

Payments are processed by Stripe. We do not receive, store, or have access to your payment card number, CVV, bank account number, or similar payment credentials. Stripe handles payment information according to its own privacy policy.

Contact Us

For questions about this policy, contact rompfortherescues@gmail.com.

This policy applies to information collected by Romp for the Rescues, LLC through rompfortherescues.org.
