Privacy Policy

Romp for the Rescues, LLC
Effective date: August 31, 2026
Last updated: August 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Romp for the Rescues, LLC (“we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, and protects personal information when you register, volunteer, make a payment, or otherwise use our accounts and related services at rompfortherescues.org.

In short: We collect your name and email address. Phone information is required for volunteers and optional for registrations. We use this information to send you email notifications about activity on our accounts and to keep a copy of the related email so we can verify that you registered or volunteered to help with one or more events. We do not share this information with anyone outside our organization. Payment information is collected and processed by Stripe on our behalf. We have no access to that payment information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following personal information:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number — required if you volunteer; optional if you register for an event

We do not collect payment card numbers, bank account details, or other payment credentials. We also do not collect additional personal information such as physical addresses, government identifiers, or location data for the purposes described in this policy.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your name, email address, and phone number (when provided or required) solely for the following purposes:

  • To send you email notifications of your activity on our accounts.
  • To keep a copy of the email associated with your registration or volunteer signup so we can verify that you registered or volunteered to help with one or more events.
  • For volunteers, to contact you by phone when needed about duties, scheduling, or event help.

If you register and choose not to provide a phone number, we will communicate with you by email only. If you volunteer, a phone number is required so we can reach you about volunteer activity.

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, sale of data, or unrelated marketing.

3. Payments Processed by Stripe

If you make a payment in connection with our accounts or events, all payment information is collected on our behalf by Stripe, who processes your payments. We in no way have any access to that payment information.

Stripe acts as an independent payment processor. Stripe’s collection and use of payment information is governed by Stripe’s own privacy policy and terms, available at https://stripe.com/privacy. We may receive from Stripe only limited non-sensitive status information needed to confirm that a payment succeeded or failed (for example, that a transaction was completed). We do not receive, store, or have access to your card number, CVV, bank account number, or similar payment credentials.

4. Sharing of Information

Your name, email address, and phone number are not shared in any way with anyone outside our organization. Access is limited to personnel within our organization who need the information to send activity notifications, reach volunteers, and verify registration or volunteer participation for events.

Payment information is not shared by us because we do not receive or hold it. That information is provided by you directly to Stripe.

5. Retention

We retain your name, email address, phone number (when provided), and a copy of the related registration or volunteer email for as long as reasonably necessary to verify that you registered or volunteered for one or more events and to provide the notification and contact services described above. When that purpose no longer applies, we delete or securely de-identify the information, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

Payment information is retained by Stripe according to Stripe’s policies, not by us.

6. Security

We take reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect your name, email address, and phone number against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. No method of electronic storage is completely secure, but we limit access to this information to authorized members of our organization.

Payment security is handled by Stripe. Because we have no access to your payment information, we do not store or transmit those details on our systems.

7. Your Choices and Rights

You may request that we update your name, email address, or phone number, stop sending activity notifications, or delete the information we hold about you, subject to any record we must keep to verify a past registration or volunteer commitment where legally required. To make a request about information we hold, contact us using the information below.

If you registered without a phone number, you may add one later. If you volunteered, a phone number is required; you may update that number, but we need a working phone number on file while you remain a volunteer.

Requests about payment card or bank details should be directed to Stripe, since we do not have access to that information.

8. Children

Our services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect name, email, or phone information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected such information, please contact us so we can delete it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will change when we do. Continued use of our accounts after an update means you acknowledge the revised policy.
