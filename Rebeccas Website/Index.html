Our Partner Charities

Donate directly to these amazing organizations — every dollar goes straight to the animals.

Blessed Paws Animal Rescue

Blessed Paws Animal Rescue saves dogs of all ages, sizes, and breeds, to protect them from the life of being abandoned, abused or neglected. We strive to provide a positive change for animal welfare in a variety of ways. We are 100% volunteer and foster based!

Visit Website Donate Now

Wild Whiskers

We rewrite futures through Trap-Neuter-Vaccinate-Return, creating thriving neighborhoods for cats and people alike. Founded by passionate advocate Brittany Mason, we believe Trap-Neuter-Vaccinate-Return (TNVR) is the only compassionate and effective solution for these cats.
