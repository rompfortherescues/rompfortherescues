I have reserved a domain name with Cloudflare.  It is "RompfortheRescues.org".  If possible, I would like to use Cloudfare exclusively for this project.

I would like a web site that handles an organization that puts on events to help fund a number of charities that deal with animals.

In addition to hosting events, links to affiliated charities are on the page to encourage donations directly to the charities.

Each of the events raises funds to support one or more charities.  Each Event will have a registration to collect the person(s) email and send them an email receipt (once their payment is accepted) to bring to the event.  This email should be from "donotreply@RompfortheRescues.org" and have a cc to RompfortheRescues@gmail.com.  The email has event data in the body. No information is stored.

Payments will be handled by Stripe.  For now just make a demo version.

Volunteers may "register" to sign up for general or specific duties on each event.  There is no fee to volunteer, but an email receipt should be sent to the volunteer from "donotreply@RompfortheRescues.org" with a cc to "rompfortherescues@gmail.com". Registration for volunteers should optionally collect event, duty preference and contact info, including telephone number (optional) on volunteer to include in the email. Information collected is not stored, but is included in the email.  We will get attendance numbers from the cc's email.

Emails will be sent using an existing Resend account.

Each of the Charities can receive donations, or donations can be made for administration.
Donations for specific charities will be accomplished by linking to their appropriate web pages for donations (see XML file <PayLink>.
Donations use the Payment Methods.
